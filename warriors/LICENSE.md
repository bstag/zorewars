# Warrior licenses

## Warriors written for ZoreWars: CC0 1.0

The warriors in this folder (and its `javascript/`, `python/` and `plane/` subfolders), in `examples/`, and in `test/fixtures/` were written for ZoreWars, except those listed below. They are dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): copy, change, share and enter them in tournaments without asking or giving credit. Credit is appreciated, though.

## Classic warriors adapted from their authors

These are adaptations of published classics, included for teaching with credit to their authors. They are **not** covered by the CC0 dedication or by the project's Apache-2.0 license; any rights belong to their original authors. The converted versions in `javascript/` and `python/` are the same programs.

| Warrior | Files | Original |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Imp | `imp.red`, `javascript/imp.js`, `python/imp.py` | A. K. Dewdney, *Scientific American*, May 1984 |
| Dwarf | `dwarf.red`, `dwarf.js`, `dwarf.py`, `javascript/dwarf.js`, `python/dwarf.py`, `examples/dwarf.py`, `examples/dwarf.cpp` | A. K. Dewdney, *Scientific American*, May 1984 |
| Mice | `mice.red`, `javascript/mice.js`, `python/mice.py` | Chip Wendell, first ICWS tournament, 1986 |
| Red Queen | `redqueen.red`, `javascript/redqueen.js`, `python/redqueen.py` | The body of Mice (Chip Wendell, 1986) unchanged, beside Gatekeeper (ZoreWars, CC0). The ZoreWars evolver came upon the combination. |

Other warriors whose descriptions say "after …" (Gemini, Imp Ring, Silk, Seeker, One-Shot and others) were written from scratch for ZoreWars. They follow published *ideas*, not code, and are CC0.

## Hall of Fame originals

The famous warriors listed in the Warrior Encyclopedia are **not** in this repository. ZoreWars fetches them from their public archives when a player imports one, and they stay in that player's browser. Their rights belong to their authors.
