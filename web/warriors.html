How many warriors exist?

Thousands. Core War has been played since 1984, and players have published their programs to newsgroups, tournament archives and "hills" (standing tournaments) ever since. The two big public archives:

5,444entries on the Koenigstuhl "infinite hills", where nothing is ever pushed off. Some warriors appear on more than one hill.
Koenigstuhl
1,106of them on the ICWS'94 "no P-space" hill, the closest match to ZoreWars' rules.
94nop hill
1,689warriors in Planar's (Damien Doligez's) archive, collected from the 1980s and 1990s.
Planar's archive
85issues of the Core Warrior newsletter (1995–2003), full of strategy write-ups.
Core Warrior

Almost all of them belong to a handful of strategy families. This page covers those families with working examples, and the new warriors written for ZoreWars' plane and 3D battlefields.

About the code on this page. The archives don't say whether their warriors may be redistributed, so the warriors here are written for ZoreWars: our own implementations of classic ideas, with a link to the original that inspired each one, and new designs for the plane and 3D battlefields. The exceptions are three tiny classics that every Core War introduction reproduces: Imp and Dwarf (A. K. Dewdney, Scientific American, 1984) and Mice (Chip Wendell, 1986), credited to their authors and kept out of our CC0 dedication (see warriors/LICENSE.md). You can still battle the originals: Fight in the Hall of Fame (or Import… in the arena, or zorewars battle classic:thermite) fetches a warrior from its archive when you use it, and it stays in your browser. All 35 originals below load and run, including FOR loops and P-space. 34 run on the classic rules. Frontwards was written for the 94x hill (55,440 cells, P-space) and is 201 instructions long, one over that hill's limit (pMARS refuses it too), so its Fight switches to the 94x hill rules with room for 201.

Counts checked on 24 October 2026. Koenigstuhl was last updated 23 July 2026.

The most recent name is worth noticing. Arvon, credited to "Stefan Kammann & Forge-AI", topped the Koenigstuhl 94nop recursive ranking in 2026. Its strategy notes describe a quickscan whose parameters came from a data-driven coverage model, followed by a silk paper and a stone. AI-assisted warriors are already competing at the top: exactly the kind of loop the ZoreWars CLI is built for.
