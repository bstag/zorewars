## Lesson for entry selection

At each step, the official push-off rule multiplies average points
against the remaining opponents by the SD distance to the nearest
remaining warrior. A larger distance raises the product for a given
average at that step. The warrior with the lowest product is eliminated,
then the averages and distances are recalculated. Each row shows its
values at its own elimination step. Giant Alien Force ranks 7th with
a product of 842.664 and Spyglass ranks 20th with 1,725.954. Each
product reflects a different set of remaining opponents.
[ZoreWars implementation of the official rule](../src/engine/pushoff.js)

The tournament recap reported that Amethystine Python scored well under
every scoring format tried. It also attributed the low push-off places
of Rodents and Hell Kitty to their matchup scores being close to those
of bombing papers such as R.O.U.S. The recap said both would have placed
high by ordinary round-robin points. For next year, compare full-field
round-robin rank with push-off rank using the same matchup matrix, and
check which opponents have the closest score profiles.

Score candidate pairs together on the target field, including any
available tournament entrants. Use `--top 64` to reproduce the LP or
94x benchmark field size. Save the round-robin matrix with
`--save <file>` and compare averages and nearest neighbors on that
fixed roster. Then rerank the saved results with each candidate
entered alone and with both
entered together, removing the omitted warrior's row and column for the
solo replay. Compare the final places relative to the same reference
opponents across fresh seeds and a held-out roster. Identifying each
warrior's closest competitors or measuring how its teammate affected
its benchmark rank requires the match matrix.
