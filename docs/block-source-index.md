## Reviewed public anatomy

### [MICE](https://gallium.inria.fr/~doligez/corewar/rc/MICE.txt)

source-reviewed; not behavior-tested. Maps to catalog candidate `paper-copy`.

- Lines 11–12: Private counter and initialization.
- Lines 13–14: Backward copy loop.
- Lines 15–17: Launch copy, advance destination and restart.
- Lines 18–18: Destination pointer storage.

Copy extent includes cells beyond the visible body; preserve initialization, both pointers and child entry together. Do not infer copy length from the number of source instructions.

### [Iron Gate 1.5](https://gallium.inria.fr/~doligez/corewar/rc/IronGate%5B2%5D.txt)

source-reviewed; not behavior-tested. Maps to catalog candidate `compare-scan`.

- Lines 6–7: Coupled spacing constants.
- Lines 9–12: Compare scan and decrement stream.
- Lines 13–16: Attack and scan pointer update.
- Lines 17–21: SPL/JMP bombs and arithmetic data.

The scan and bombing sections modify each other through indirect references. Preserve pointer coupling and spacing constraints before trying to separate the attack.

### [Silk Warrior 1.3](https://gallium.inria.fr/~doligez/corewar/rc/SilkWarrior1.3.txt)

source-reviewed; not behavior-tested. Maps to catalog candidate `silk-launch`.

- Lines 19–23: Process launcher.
- Lines 25–26: Child launch and copying.
- Lines 27–31: Bombing, continuation and gate data.

Launcher scheduling, paper pointer side effects and gate data belong to one recipe. Its 8000-cell assertion is a real compatibility requirement, not merely an annotation.

### [Torch t18](https://gallium.inria.fr/~doligez/corewar/rc/Torcht18.txt)

source-reviewed; not behavior-tested. Maps to catalog candidate `relocate-copy`.

- Lines 13–24: Two-process boot and pointer erasure.
- Lines 29–35: Clear/gate state and padding.
- Lines 36–40: Paired incendiary bombs and self-modifying transition.
- Lines 41–46: Clear and second bomb component.
- Lines 47–55: Padding and decoy data.

Booted regions are noncontiguous and share two processes. This boot cannot replace the catalog single-process copy; bomb arithmetic and self-triggered handoff also need their own contracts.
