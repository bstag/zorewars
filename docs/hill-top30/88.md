# Koenigstuhl ICWS'88 hill, top 30: source-level breakdown

Method: every file `01-*.red` to `30-*.red` was read in full; lengths are from `zorewars compile --rules 88` (assembled instructions, ORG excluded). Process counts were derived by hand from the SPL trees. Items marked **(uncertain)** were inferred from the source but not traced in the simulator.

Tournament limit is 50 instructions. **Only 2 of the 30 fit as published: Eidolon (13) and Tangle Trap 3 (38).** Everything else is 55 to 100.

Quickscan families used below:

- **M-qscan** (Metcalf "36 scans in 48 instructions"): 18 `CMP` pairs + 1 `JMZ` probe, additive decoder `qslower/qslow/qfast` (3 `ADD @qN` lines that sum offsets stored in the spare B-fields of the `JMP`/`DJN` lines after each CMP; `JMP x,<q1` predecrements select the table entry), `qskip` picks the occupied half of the pair, then a 9-pass burst (`mov qbomb,@qkil / mov <..,<qfirst / sub #7,@qloop / djn qloop,#9`) of `dat <-46,<46`. 48 instructions.
- **J-qscan** (Janeczek, Quicksilver '88 form, `qs`/`qd` constants): 12 CMP pairs + `JMZ`, decoder `qa3/qa2/qa1` (same additive idea, tables in B-fields of `qt1..qt3`/`qs1..qs3`), `cmp @qp,..; cmp @0,0; add #qd,qp` picks the side, 6-pass burst stepping 9 with two writes a pass 43 apart. 36 to 37 instructions. "Trimmed" versions drop to 10 or 11 pairs (33 to 34 instructions).
- **q^2** (David Moore): 13 CMP pairs, decode tables hidden in B-fields of payload lines (`qt1/qt3` are the imp launcher's `spl 1` lines), attack plants an `SPL 0 / SPL -1 / JMP -2` stun pit on the hit followed by a step-10 run of `JMP` bombs (4 passes). About 45 instructions. Attack details **(uncertain)**.

## Per-warrior notes

### 1. Lithobolia C yvfp (Steve Gunnell), 99
Opening is the M-qscan cut to 13 CMP pairs with a very wide spacing (`QGAP 1168`, `qstep 2336`) and a longer burst (`qi 5`, `qr 10`: 12 passes, bomb `dat <-48,<48`). `warr` is an unbalanced SPL tree (`spl 24 / spl 12 / spl 6 ...`) with 8 leaves: seven leaves each run five lines (`mov D,<4 / mov C,<3 / mov B,<2 / mov A,<1 / jmp @0,FIRST+k*HOP`) that copy the 4-line stone through the JMP's own B-field and jump into it, landing stones at `warr+6247 + k*992`, k = 0..6; the eighth leaf enters the paper. The stone is a resin-type clear: `SPL 0` head, two MOVs that walk a pointer downward laying `mov <C,<n` instructions, and a DJN stream; measured peak is 8000 processes. The paper makes 6 processes (`spl 1 / mov -1,0 / spl 1`), copies its 12-line body 2302 away, then a 6-line tail 2949 away, then again 4368 away, and every copy ends in a `spl 0 / add #2667,1 / jmp @0,imp-2667*6` pump over one `mov 0,2667`. Ring formation of that pump with 6 lockstep processes is **uncertain**, as is the stone's exact kill mechanism. Nearest blocks: `opening-qscan-add-88`, `distributed-stones`, `silk-88`; no block does the 7-way split tree.
```
A   spl 0   , <qscan
B   mov C   , <C
C   mov <C  , <0-(HOP*2/3)      ; -661
D   djn A   , <qscan
```

### 2. Talisman (John Metcalf), 69
Full M-qscan (48). `warr` places three adjacent imp instructions at `ldist+idist`, boots the stone bomb 16 cells from the stone, then `spl 2 / spl 2 / spl 1` makes 5 processes that each copy one launcher cell and one stone cell (`mov <launch,<lboot / mov <stone-1,<sboot`); `djn @sboot,#5` sends 4 into the stone and the fifth falls through `jmp @0` into the imp launcher (the earlier review says five and one; my count of the SPL tree is 5 total). Stone: `SPL 0` head, two MOVs dropping `dat <-19,<-19` on adjacent cells (one `<`, one `@` through the same pointer), `sub #2211`, DJN stream at 2438. `time 1819` aims the first pointer so the stone hits itself after 1819 loops; what it then becomes was not traced **(uncertain)**. Pump: `spl 0 / add #2668,iptr / jmp @0,..`, 3-point. Nearest blocks: `opening-qscan-add-88`, `fanboot-stone-imp` ('94 only), `hill-stone`, `compact-imp-pump`.
```
        spl 0,             0+5
stone   mov stone+bdist-1, <hit+time*step
hit     mov stone+bdist-1, @stone
        sub #step,         @hit         ; 2211
        djn stone,         <2438
launch  spl 0,             0+5
        add #istep+1,      iptr
iptr    jmp @0,            launch+idist-istep-1
```

### 3. Freight Train v0.2 (David Moore), 74
q^2 opening (13 pairs, tables in the launcher's B-fields; on a hit it plants `jmp -2 / spl -1 / spl 0` then four step-10 passes of JMP bombs). `main`: `spl launch`, then 4 processes copy 4 stone cells to +1500 and the 3-line continuous launcher to +2004 and +6508, and all 4 enter the stone. `launch` makes 8 processes, `spl 2 / jmp @0,imp-is / add #is,-1` sends 8 to consecutive points of a 7-point spiral (`is = 1143`) and the other 8 jump into the stone (12 stone processes). `cboot` places the imp, drops the special bomb `dat <2667,<1` 7 cells from the stone, and starts both continuous launchers (`spl 0 / add #1+is,1 / jmp @0,..`), which keep feeding the same spiral. Stone step 82; the source pointer walks -2 a loop so after 2437 loops it fetches the special bomb onto its own DJN, and `add 1,-2` then advances the MOV target by 1: a forward clear. Nearest blocks: `dual-launcher` (two spirals, not two pumps), `vector-spiral`, `stone-then-clear`; q^2 opening: none.
```
stone  mov <dd+1+(2*time), 3+(step*time)
       spl  -1, <2-step
       add   1,   -2
       djn  -2, <0-step   ; gets bombed with db to start core clear
cont   spl      0,  0
       add  #1+is,  1
       jmp     @0,  cont-c1bd+(imp-main)+(6*is)
```

### 4. Resinoid (John Metcalf), 68
Full M-qscan. `spl 2 / spl 1 / spl 1` gives 6 processes; each copies one cell of the resin twice and one of the paper, then `spl @0,bpos+4000 / spl @0,bpos / jmp @0,bpos-3800`: 6 processes in each of two resin clears 4000 apart and 6 in the paper. The paper copies its 6 lines 1085 away once per generation and the parents fall into `spl 0 / spl istep+1 / mov -1,istep / mov 0,istep`, which builds 3-point imp chains in place. Resin ("clear inspired by GT"): two `SPL 0`, `mov 1,<1` lays the next line (`mov <0,<3321`) downward through its own B-field, that line moves again, DJN stream 100 below. The bombs are MOV instructions, not DATs; how it converts stuns into kills is **uncertain**. Nearest blocks: `silk-88`, `silk-imps` ('94); resin: none.
```
paper   mov    <size,    <papa
papa    spl    @0,       pstep+size     ; 1085
        spl    0,        <4000
        spl    istep+1,  <4000+istep
        mov    -1,       istep
imp     mov    0,        istep
resin   spl    0,        <resin-100
        spl    0,        <resin-100
clear   mov    1,        <1
        mov    <0,       <3321
        djn    clear,    <resin-100
```

### 5. Quicksilver '88 (Michal Janeczek), 55
J-qscan with 12 pairs (`qs 322`, `qd 161`), the bomb cell being `jmp boot,<43`, which is also the exit. Boot is 9 lines: the stone bomb goes 11 past the stone, `spl 2 / spl 1` makes 3 processes that place 3 imps and 3 launcher cells, `mov -3,0` overwrites itself with `spl 1` so the count becomes 5, five stone cells are copied, and `djn boot+sdist,#5` sends 4 to the stone and the last to the launcher. Stone: `SPL 0`, two MOVs of `dat <1,#17` (pair bombing), `add #-107`, DJN stream at 5246. Pump is the 3-line JMP/ADD type with `#2668`. No end clear. This is the smallest stone/imp on the hill: 19 instructions without the qscan. Nearest blocks: `fanboot-stone-imp` idea, `hill-stone`, `compact-imp-pump`, `opening-qscan-add-88`.
```
stone spl 0             , imp
      mov stone+dboff   , <-123
      mov stone+dboff   , @-1
      add #-107         , -2
      djn -3            , <5246
      spl 0             , 0
      add #2668         , 1
      jmp @0            , ioff-idist-2670
```

### 7. Mountain Viper (inversed), 100
J-qscan trimmed to 10 pairs (`qs 235`, `qd 624`), 12-pass burst stepping 9 with `dat <40,<-39`. Boot copies the 5-line scanner to +5828 and the 6-line clear `step+2` cells after it, forks one process into the scanner, places the `kill` DAT below the clear and then dies in `kill` (the launch image keeps no live process). Scanner: `sub inc,look / cmp a+12,a / jmp step,step / djn scan,@look`, step 23, 0.66c, and the increment cell is the `jmp` to the clear. On a hit the clear's first line copies `look` (carrying the scan address) into the clear's pointer cell, then `spl 0,<-gate / mov @cref,<ptr / mov @cref,<ptr / djn -2,<stream` lays SPLs downward from the hit. When the pointer wraps onto its own cell and then onto `cref`, `@cref` flips from the SPL to the `kill` DAT: SPL pass, then DAT pass, with `<-gate` as imp gate (my reading of the offsets, not traced). 42-line `spl -((i-1)%7),1` decoy between scanner and qscan. Nearest blocks: `one-shot`, `finisher-overwrite`, `decoys`.
```
scan    sub   inc          ,   look
look    cmp   scan0+hop    ,   scan0
inc     jmp   step         ,   step
        djn   scan         , @ look
        mov  -step-1       , @ 2
        spl   0            , <-gate
        mov @ cref         , < clast+cofs
        mov @ cref         , < clast+cofs
clast   djn  -2            , < stream
```

### 8. Zygote, hill version (Roy van Rijn), 100
J-qscan (12 pairs) at the far end of the image, 34 empty cells in between. No boot: seven `spl stone` lines put 7 processes in the stone where it stands, then `spl 1 / spl 1 / mov -1,0` makes 7 for the paper. Stone is the Quicksilver stone (step -107, `dat <1,#17`, DJN stream 2350). Paper is a 7-line looping bomber, step 7349: `add #7,bptr` doubles as the copy counter (its B-field is the source pointer; when it reaches 0 the first `add` re-arms it to 7 and the rest advance `bptr`), then one bomb row under the new copy and one bomb stream via `bptr`, bomb `dat <2667,<5334`, and `jmz paper,paper` closes the loop. Nearest blocks: `bomb-paper`, `spl-stone`/`hill-stone`.
```
paper add #7,bptr
     mov <-1,<1
     spl @0,7349
     mov bomb,<-1
     mov bomb,<bptr
     jmz paper,paper
bomb  dat <2667,<5334
```

### 9. Elemental Dust 2 (John Metcalf), 100
No quickscan. The first 11 lines copy the vampire (4), fang, increment and trap (4) 3054 cells away keeping their spacing, and fork into it; then the imp is copied to +6619 and a binary tree (`spl 8 / spl 4 / spl 2` ...) sends 6 processes to `imp + k*2667`, k = 0..5 (two turns of a 3-point spiral) and a second process to the vampire. Vampire: `SPL 0`, `mov fang,@fang`, `add inc,@vloop` (`dat <-644,<644` keeps the fang's A pointing at the trap), DJN stream. Trap: prisoners run `mov bomb,<vampire-27 / spl trap / jmp @trap+1`, so they clear the core with `dat <2667,..` for the owner. All gaps and the rest of the 100 cells are `dat #trap,#1` decoy. Real code is 26 lines, so a no-decoy version fits 50. Nearest blocks: `vampire-trap`, `binary-twin-imp`/`imp-ring`, `decoys`.
```
vampire spl 0,         <2-fang
vloop   mov fang,      @fang
        add inc,       @vloop
        djn vloop,     <dpos
fang    jmp trap-vloop-step,<vloop-fang+step
trap    mov bomb,      <vampire-27
        spl trap
        jmp @trap+1
```

### 10. Der Zweite Blitzkrieg 2021 (Mike Nonemacher), 75

When the stone overwrites its own `jmp -2` it falls into `mov @PTR,<stone+INCR / jmp -1`, a clear whose source pointer selects first `spl 0` then the `dat <GVAL-1` gate: two passes, ending as a gate (per strategy line; transition not traced, **uncertain**). ## Patterns

- **The opening is the length problem.** 24 of 30 carry a 33 to 48-instruction quickscan; with it, no body fits in 50 (Talisman would have 2 instructions left). A 50-instruction entry must either drop the qscan (as 9, 16, 21, 23, 27, 28 do; four of them still sit in the top 30 on body strength) or use a 4 to 6-pair version of about 15 to 20 instructions (`opening-quickscan-88`, or a short `opening-qscan-add-88`).
- **Bodies are small.** Without qscan, decoy and padding: Murmur 15, Sorcery 18, Quicksilver 19, Resinoid 20, Talisman 21, Triangular Sun 21, Permafrost 23, Whirl 23, Torment 25, Into the Unknown 25, Monolith 27 (with boot), Corona Discharger 28, Eye of the Storm 29. So the realistic 50-line shapes are body + short qscan, or body + 20 to 25 lines of decoy.
- **Boot is near universal (27/30) and cheap**: parallel processes each copy one cell through a B-field that then serves as the jump vector (`mov <src,<dst` xN, `spl @0`/`jmp @0`), 5 to 10 instructions. `mov -1,0` between `spl 1` lines is the standard way to get 5, 6, 7, 9 or 15 processes. Scanner boots let the boot process die so nothing live stays in the image.
- **Stone/imp is the modal design (9 of 30, 4 in the top 10)**: 4 to 8 stone processes behind `SPL 0`, pair bombing with a DJN stream, plus a 3-line JMP/ADD pump on one process. 3-point imps dominate (12 of 15); the three 7-point users (3, 10, 26) are the answer to the many `dat <2667` bombs and 3-stream stones aimed at 3-point spirals.
- **Papers here do not loop**: 7 of 9 are straight-line `mov <n,<p / spl @0` chains of 2 or 3 stages that end in something else (imp tail, resin, mini clear). They are booted in pairs or next to a stone and run 6 to 9 processes. Only Zygote and The Next Step bomb.
- **Anti-imp measures are everywhere**: `dat <2667,..` bombs (11 warriors), DJN streams in nearly every stone and scanner, gates in the one-shots. An entry relying on a plain 3-point spiral for ties should expect it to be attacked.
- **Scanners all stun first**: three B-scanners (SPL 0 + JMP/JMN -1 pairs at 0.5c), three CMP scanners with SPL wipes (0.66c), two one-shots with SPL-then-DAT clears. Six of the eight spend 27 to 80 instructions on SPL decoys; under a 50 limit that decoy shrinks to 20 or less, and Eidolon shows the scanner still works without it.
- **Self-timing by self-hit** replaces counters: stones (`time` constants), Semiopacity/Janus (pointer starts at `-step*time`) and the one-shots switch phase when their own pointer reaches a chosen cell of their own code. It costs no instructions, which matters at 50.
- **Field reuse is the house style**: increments that are live instructions (Monolith's `add step,step`, Snake3's pit line, Freight Train's DJN), boot pointers that are the bomb's B-field (Stasis Prime, Prometheus), qscan tables in payload B-fields (q^2), a JMP bomb that is also the exit (Quicksilver). Our blocks tend to keep these cells separate, so hand-merging after `algebra build` is where the last 5 to 10 instructions come from.
- **What a 50-instruction entry gives up**: the 18-pair qscan (keep 4 to 6 pairs or none), large decoys, the second payload of the three-part designs (Lithobolia's seven stones, Freight Train's dual pumps, Janus's second scanner, Zygote/Copper Sputterer's spacer), and in-place launch with padding. What it can keep unchanged: any single stone/imp, twin paper, vampire or scanner body from this list.
